The Problem

Manual reconciliation is slow.
Automated guessing is worse.

Finance teams reconcile settlements against bank statements and invoices that arrive with missing references, vendor-name drift, fee deductions, duplicates, and partial payments. A system that force-matches ambiguous records doesn’t save time — it moves the risk downstream.

Core Principle

“Precision is non-negotiable.
Recall is negotiable.”

One wrong auto-close in finance costs more than ten escalations that route to a human reviewer. The system is calibrated to minimize false matches, even at the cost of a higher exception rate.

High confidence
AUTO_CLOSE
Score ≥ 0.90, no tie, both counterparts present. Written to the decision store.
Ambiguous
ESCALATE
Tied candidates, low confidence, or missing evidence. Routes to human review with full context.
Insufficient evidence
UNRESOLVED
Invoice or bank counterpart not found. Cannot form a three-way match.
How It Decides
01

Normalize

Settlement, bank, and invoice records are parsed into one canonical schema. Amounts, dates, and vendor names are cleaned of formatting noise before any matching begins.

02

Score & Block

A weighted scoring matrix ranks candidates: 0.35×reference, 0.25×amount, 0.20×vendor similarity, 0.20×date proximity. Blocking keys limit candidate search to O(n·k) — not O(n²).

deterministic · no LLM
03

Policy Gate

AUTO_CLOSE is policy-authorized closure in this sandbox, not a bank posting. ESCALATE means a candidate exists but evidence is insufficient (including a nearby decoy). UNRESOLVED means no valid counterpart was established.

matcher/policy.py → never_auto_close_on_tie()
04

Explain & Audit

Escalated records with low confidence are passed to the LLM for a human-readable explanation. It recommends — it never writes a decision. Every outcome, matched or not, is logged with an immutable evidence snapshot.

AI-assisted · explain-only · full audit trail
Decision Engine

The LLM is downstream of the policy gate.
It never sets the decision.

The architecture makes this structurally impossible to circumvent. The matcher scores candidates. The policy gate decides. Only then — and only for escalations — does the LLM get called to explain the context to a human reviewer.
